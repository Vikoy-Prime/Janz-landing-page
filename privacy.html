Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ask Me Janice handles information provided through this website.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, cellphone number, and message. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about the services you asked about.

Please do not submit sensitive information.

Do not send passwords, bank or account details, government ID numbers, medical information, or other highly sensitive personal or financial information through the website contact form.

Contact form processing

Contact-form submissions are processed using Formspree. Information submitted through the form may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Formspree as part of delivering the message.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as visits, pages viewed, device information, and similar traffic data. Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose to allow analytics. If you choose “No thanks,” Analytics will not load. Your preference is stored in your browser so the website can remember your choice.

Public contact information

The website may display Janice Balanon’s approved professional contact information, including Facebook, email, and cellphone number, so visitors can contact her directly.

How information is used

  • To respond to questions and service inquiries.
  • To communicate about financial planning or protection-related services requested by the visitor.
  • To maintain and improve the website.
  • To help protect the website from spam or misuse.

Third-party services

This website may interact with third-party services such as Formspree, Google Analytics, and Facebook. Their own privacy practices may apply when you use those services or follow links to them.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the contact form and instead contact Janice directly through the published Facebook, email, or cellphone contact options.

Questions about your information

For questions about information submitted through this website, contact Janice through the email or cellphone number published on the main website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features, contact methods, or third-party services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
